Palov recipe

Home

Description

Palov is a traditional dish made with rice, meat, and vegetables. It is a staple in many Central Asian countries and is known for its rich flavor and aroma.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg rice
  • 1 kg meat
  • 2 onions
  • 3 carrots
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 cup of oil
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp turmeric

Instructions

  1. Wash the rice and soak it in water for 30 minutes.
  2. In a large pot, heat the oil and add the chopped onions. Cook until golden brown.
  3. Add the meat and cook until browned.
  4. Add the carrots and spices, and cook for another 5 minutes.
  5. Add the soaked rice and enough water to cover it. Bring to a boil.
  6. Reduce heat, cover, and simmer for 20-25 minutes or until rice is cooked.
  7. Fluff with a fork and serve hot.

Notes

You can add raisins or dried fruits for a sweeter version.

Serve with salad or yogurt on the side.

Tips

  • Use basmati rice for better flavor.
  • Let the dish rest for 10 minutes before serving for better texture.
  • Adjust spices according to your taste.
